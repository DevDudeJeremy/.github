# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

DevDudeJeremy projects should be welcoming places for thoughtful technical collaboration. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving useful feedback about the work rather than the person;
- making space for different experience levels and perspectives;
- accepting correction gracefully;
- respecting privacy, attribution, and clearly stated project boundaries; and
- prioritizing the health of the community over winning an argument.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and other degrading or exclusionary conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces and when someone is representing a DevDudeJeremy project in public. It covers repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and related community interactions.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact Jeremy privately through [devdudejeremy.com](https://devdudejeremy.com). Do not use a public issue for a sensitive conduct report.

Reports will be reviewed with care for context, privacy, and the safety of everyone involved. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior does not meet this standard.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
